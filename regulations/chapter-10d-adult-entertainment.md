# CHAPTER 10D - ADULT ENTERTAINMENT BUSINESSES

## Article I. Purpose and Definitions

### 10D.1 Purpose

The purpose of this chapter is to prevent community-wide adverse secondary effects that can be generated by the unregulated operation of adult entertainment businesses. These adverse secondary effects include, but are not limited to: depreciation of property values; increased vacancy rates in residential and commercial areas; increased criminal activity; increased litter, noise, and vandalism; and interference with the enjoyment of residential property in the vicinity of such businesses.

### 10D.2 Definitions

**(a) "Adult entertainment business"** includes:
1. **Adult arcade** - Establishment showing films/images characterized by specified sexual activities for 5 or fewer persons
2. **Adult cabaret** - Establishment presenting live performances characterized by specified sexual activities or featuring semi-nude persons
3. **Adult motion picture theater** - Establishment showing films characterized by specified sexual activities
4. **Adult retail store** - Establishment offering adult entertainment material/merchandise for sale or rent

**(b) "Adult entertainment material"** - Audio/visual representations characterized by specified sexual activities or specified anatomical areas

**(c) "Adult entertainment merchandise"** - Devices designed for stimulation of human genital organs or sadomasochistic activity

**(d) "Director"** - Police chief of the City of Fairfield or designee

**(e) "Entertainer"** - Person who performs at an adult entertainment business for consideration

**(f) "Hearing officer"** - City manager or designee

**(g) "Regular and substantial course of conduct"** means:
- 30%+ of stock-in-trade is adult material/merchandise
- 30%+ of display area is adult material/merchandise
- Adult entertainment on 4+ days within any 30-day period
- 30%+ of gross receipts from adult material/services

**(h) "Semi-nude"** - Clothing covers only genitals, pubic region, buttocks, areola and nipple

**(i) "Specified anatomical areas"** - Less than completely covered genitals, buttocks, female breast below areola; turgid male genitals

**(j) "Specified sexual activities"** - Erotic touching, sex acts, masturbation (actual or simulated)

## Article II. Adult Entertainment Regulatory Permit

### 10D.3 Permit required

Unlawful to operate adult entertainment business without obtaining and maintaining an adult entertainment regulatory permit.

### 10D.4 Permit application

Owner must be 18+ years of age. Application requires:
- Completed application signed by applicant and property owner/lessor
- Fingerprints
- Two color photographs
- Written business description
- Site plan with interior floor plan and parking
- Certification statement
- Nonrefundable application fee

### 10D.5 Approval or denial

Director shall approve or deny within 30 city business days. Decisions may be appealed to hearing officer.

### 10D.6 Nontransferable

Permits are location-specific and nontransferable.

### 10D.7 Location criteria

Must comply with Section 25.20(a)(13)(A) of Chapter 25.

### 10D.8 Design standards

- No temporary/portable structures
- Enclosed trash dumpsters
- Landscaping height limits (30" max, trees 6'+ foliage)
- Illuminated parking (1 footcandle average)
- Sound-absorbing insulation
- Indoor areas open to management view
- Minimum illumination: Retail 20fc, Cabarets 5fc (1.25fc during performances), Arcades 10fc
- Separate restrooms (male/female)
- Manager's station with unobstructed view
- Adult cabarets: separate dressing rooms, separated stage access

### 10D.9 Performance standards

- No observation from outside of adult material/activities
- Doors/windows not propped open
- No persons under 18
- Security surveillance for parking
- Security guards required (1 minimum; 2 if occupancy >50)
- Hours: Cannot operate 2am-8am
- Adult arcades: 1 person per booth, no loitering, clean facilities
- Adult cabarets: stage 18"+ above floor, 6'+ from patrons, no physical contact

### 10D.10-12 Entertainer requirements

- No entertainers without valid permits
- Maintain register of entertainers
- Display permit conspicuously

### 10D.13 Inspections

Unscheduled inspections allowed during business hours.

### 10D.14-16 Permit term and renewal

- Valid 24 months
- Renewal application 30 days before expiration
- Requirements are permit conditions

## Article III. Adult Entertainer Permit

### 10D.17-18 Permit required and application

Must be 18+ years of age. Application requires personal information, criminal history, fingerprints, photographs, and fee.

### 10D.19 Grounds for denial

- False statements
- Under 18
- Business lacks regulatory permit

### 10D.20-24 Permit terms

- Approval within 4 business days
- Nontransferable
- Must be available for inspection
- Valid 12 months
- Renewal 30 days before expiration

---

## Article IV. Suspension and Revocation

### 10D.25 Grounds

Regulatory permit: false statements, noncompliance, criminal activity, felony/misdemeanor in business conduct, failure to abide by disciplinary action, unauthorized enlargement.

Entertainer permit: false statements, unlawful sexual activities on premises, conduct requiring sex offender registration, lewdness/prostitution, obscenity violations.

### 10D.26 Procedure

Written notice with hearing date (10 days prior). Hearings allow evidence, counsel, cross-examination. Penalties: warning, suspension (up to 6 months), or revocation.

---

## Article V. Appeals

### 10D.27-33 Appeal process

- Written appeal within 10 calendar days
- Director's decision stayed during appeal
- Hearing within 30 business days
- Burden on appellant to prove decision unreasonable
- Decision within 45 business days
- Judicial review per CCP Section 1094.5

---

## Article VI. Miscellaneous

### 10D.34 Existing businesses

May continue without compliance if legally operating before effective date and not substantially enlarged.

### 10D.35 Regulations nonexclusive

Compliance does not excuse noncompliance with other Code provisions.

### 10D.36 Conflicts

This chapter prevails over conflicting Code provisions.
