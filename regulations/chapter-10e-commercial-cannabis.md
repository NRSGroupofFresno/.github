# CHAPTER 10E - COMMERCIAL CANNABIS BUSINESSES

## 10E.1 Purpose and Intent

It is the purpose and intent of this Chapter to implement the provisions of the Medicinal and Adult Use Cannabis Regulation and Safety Act (Business and Professions Code Section 26001 et seq., "MAUCRSA") to accommodate the needs of medically-ill persons in need of cannabis for medicinal purposes as recommended by their health care provider(s). It is also the purpose and intent of this Chapter to provide access to adult-use cannabis for persons aged 21 and over as authorized by the Control, Tax & Regulate the Adult Use Cannabis Act ("AUMA" or "Proposition 64" approved by California voters in 2016), while imposing reasonable regulations to protect the City's residents, neighborhoods, and businesses from any harmful impacts.

## 10E.2 Legal Authority

Pursuant to Sections 5 and 7 of Article XI of the California Constitution, the provisions of MAUCRSA, and any subsequent State legislation or regulations, the City of Fairfield is authorized to adopt ordinances that establish standards, requirements and regulations for the licensing and permitting of commercial cannabis activity.

## 10E.3 Commercial Cannabis Activities Prohibited Unless Specifically Authorized

Except as specifically authorized by this Chapter, the commercial cultivation, manufacture, processing, storing, laboratory testing, labeling, sale, delivery, distribution, and transportation of cannabis or cannabis products is expressly prohibited in the City.

## 10E.4 Compliance with State and Local Laws

It is the responsibility of the owner(s) of a commercial cannabis business to ensure that it operates in a manner that complies with all applicable State and local laws, any regulations promulgated thereunder, and any licensing or permit requirements or conditions of approval.

## 10E.5 Definitions

Key definitions include:

- **"Bureau"** - Bureau of Cannabis Control with the California Department of Consumer Affairs
- **"Commercial cannabis business"** - Any business engaging in medicinal or adult-use commercial cannabis activity
- **"Commercial cannabis business permit"** - Regulatory permit issued by the City pursuant to this Chapter
- **"Retailer"** - As defined in Section 26070(a)(1) of the Business and Professions Code
- **"Storefront Retailer"** - Retailer with physical premises selling cannabis goods to customers at its premises or by delivery
- **"Non-Storefront retailer"** - Retailer selling cannabis goods exclusively through delivery

## 10E.6 Commercial Cannabis Business Permit Required; Number of Permits

**(a)** No person may engage in any commercial cannabis business within the City unless they have:
1. A valid commercial cannabis business permit from the City
2. A valid State license
3. Current compliance with all applicable State and local laws

**(b)** Maximum permits allowed:
- Retailer: maximum of two (2)
- Manufacturing: maximum of one (1)
- Testing Laboratory: unlimited
- Commercial cultivation: zero (0)
- Microbusiness: zero (0)
- Distribution: zero (0)

## 10E.7 Persons Prohibited from Holding a Permit

Any person shall be prohibited from holding a permit if:
1. Under the age of 21
2. Had a permit revoked by any cannabis licensing authority
3. Was conducting commercial cannabis activity in violation of this Code
4. Delinquent in payment of taxes/fees
5. As of June 1, 2020, was conducting commercial cannabis activity in violation of law

## 10E.8 Application Procedures

Applications must include:
- Location information and Zoning Verification Letter
- Background investigation of owners
- State license description
- State Seller's Permit
- Operations description
- Security Plan
- Fire Safety Plan
- Storage and Transportation Plan
- Community Relations Plan
- Application fee

## 10E.9 Permittee Selection Process

The City Council determines approval based on:
1. Location and site characteristics consistent with standards
2. Provisions to ensure no excessive demands for police/public services
3. Benefit to the City

## 10E.10 Expiration and Renewal

- Initial permit: valid 12 months
- First renewal: valid 12 months
- Subsequent renewals: valid 36 months
- Renewal applications: filed 60-90 days before expiration

## 10E.11 Suspension and Revocation

The City Manager may temporarily suspend permits for imminent threats to public health, safety or welfare. The City Council may revoke permits for violations of State or local law.

## 10E.12 Effect of State License Suspension

Suspension or expiration of State license immediately suspends the City permit. Revocation of State license automatically revokes the City permit.

## 10E.13 Appeal Process

Appeals must be filed within 10 calendar days of the City Manager's decision. The City Council conducts a de novo hearing.

## 10E.14-15 Changes and Transfers

Permits are nontransferable to another location. Changes in ownership require new applications and City Council approval for substantial changes (>51% ownership change).

## 10E.16-17 Business License and Building Permits

Commercial cannabis businesses must obtain City business license and pass mandatory building inspections before commencing operations.

## 10E.18 Location and Design Requirements

**Zoning Districts:**
- Manufacturing/Testing Labs: IL, IG, IBP
- Retail: IL, CR, CS, CT, CM, IBP, HWT (excluding Travis Blvd/Pennsylvania Ave/Gateway Blvd area)

**Distance Requirements:**
- Minimum 600 feet from schools (K-12), child day care centers, and youth centers

## 10E.19 Limitations on City's Liability

Permittees must:
- Execute indemnification agreement
- Maintain required insurance
- Reimburse City for legal costs

## 10E.20 Records and Recordkeeping

Businesses must maintain:
- Accurate electronic books and records
- Annual financial audits
- Register of owners/employees
- Inventory control and reporting system
- Track and trace compliance

## 10E.21 Employee Background Checks

Required before hire or within 30 days of permit issuance. Certain felony convictions are grounds for denial of employment.

## 10E.22 Security Measures

Required security includes:
- Perimeter fencing and exterior lighting
- Limited access areas
- Secured storage (UL-rated safes/vaults)
- 24-hour 4K surveillance cameras
- Entry/exit sensors
- Panic buttons
- Alarm systems
- 24-hour security personnel
- Network security (UL-certified)

## 10E.23 Restriction on Alcohol & Tobacco

No alcohol or tobacco sales or consumption on premises. No cannabis consumption on premises.

## 10E.24 General Operating Requirements

- Operating hours as specified in permit
- No visible cannabis from exterior
- Point-of-sale inventory tracking
- Emergency contact information required
- Signage regulations
- No persons under 21 (except medical patients 18+ with valid recommendation)
- Odor control systems required

## 10E.25 Operating Requirements for Storefront Retailers

- Age/documentation verification required
- Uniformed licensed security personnel
- Limited daily inventory in retail area
- Locked restrooms
- No drive-through service
- Child-resistant packaging
- Limited-access areas with escort requirements
- Operating hours: 6:00 a.m. - 10:00 p.m.

## 10E.26 Operating Requirements for Non-Storefront Retailers

- Age verification for deliveries
- Operating hours: 6:00 a.m. - 10:00 p.m.
- Vehicle information required
- No delivery to public property
- No kiosk/technology platforms facilitating sales at non-permitted locations

## 10E.27 Operating Requirements for Testing Labs

- Compliance with Business and Professions Code Section 26100
- ISO/IEC 17025 accreditation required
- Destroy non-compliant batches (unless remediation possible)
- Chain of custody protocols
- May test samples from qualified patients with valid recommendation

## 10E.28 Operating Requirements for Manufacturing Facilities

- Compressed gas limits per Fire Department approval
- Allowed extraction methods: heat, screens, presses, steam distillation, ice water, ethanol, CO2
- Professional closed-loop systems required with engineer certification
- Fire Department approval required
- Standard operating procedures and training required

## 10E.29 Operating Requirements for Out-of-City Delivery Services

Required before delivering within City:
- City business license and cannabis delivery permit
- Evidence of valid State license
- Vehicle information
- Copy of permit retained by drivers

## 10E.30 Promulgation of Regulations

City Manager authorized to establish additional rules with City Council approval.

## 10E.31 Community Relations

- Community relations representative required
- Regular meetings with City Manager during first year
- City-approved public outreach and educational program required

## 10E.32-38 Enforcement

- Fees deemed debt to City
- Permittee responsible for all violations
- City may conduct 24-hour notice inspections
- Violations declared public nuisance
- Each violation is separate offense
- Criminal penalties: up to $1,000 fine and/or 6 months imprisonment
- Remedies are cumulative
